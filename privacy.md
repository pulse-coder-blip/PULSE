Pulse Privacy Policy
Pulse is a personal dashboard that displays WHOOP data (recovery, strain, sleep, heart rate, HRV and related metrics) for its owner.

What we access: With your permission through WHOOP's login, Pulse reads your recovery, cycle, sleep and profile data from the WHOOP API.

How it's used: Only to display your own metrics to you. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage: Access tokens are stored on the server running Pulse. Health data is held in temporary memory and not kept in a database.

Your control: You can revoke access at any time in your WHOOP account settings, and delete the stored tokens by removing the tokens.json file.
